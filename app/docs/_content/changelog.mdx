## Highlights

- **v2.0.0**: Physical iPhone and iPad support, iPhone Duo simulator support with `displays`, `hinge` and `rotate`, agent mode (`--agent`) for AI agents, `--output json` with typed error codes and JSON Schemas, `axe settings`, `axe draw`, long press, double tap and two-finger tap on `tap`, scroll-until-visible, pinch, rotate and two-finger gestures, `shutdown`, `clipboard copy`, `list-devices`, `doctor`, the experimental `axe agent` command, plugin marketplaces for the skill, and much faster simulator input. See [What's New in AXe 2](/docs/whats-new).
- **v1.8.0**: Xcode 27 and iOS 27 simulator automation, with Xcode 26 still supported.
- **v1.7.0**: `slider --id/--label --value` with value verification, `drag`, orientation-aware coordinates, and switch activation through `--tap-style`.
- **v1.6.0**: `--value` targeting for `tap`, `--element-type`, and `--wait-timeout` with `--poll-interval`.
- **v1.5.0**: `axe batch` and `axe init`.
- **v1.3.0**: `key-combo`.
- **v1.2.0**: Screenshots, and tap by label or accessibility ID.
- **v1.1.0**: Video streaming and recording.
